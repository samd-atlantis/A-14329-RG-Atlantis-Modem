A-14329-MB00 firmware bring-up plan

1. Record fitted HLK router SKU, flash/RAM and modem part number/firmware.
2. Back up each module's own flash/calibration/MAC data privately. Verify UART0
   console and U-Boot recovery before changing firmware. Do not erase bootloader.
3. Build the pinned OpenWrt development source on Linux after confirming target
   partitions and memory. Inspect image size and supported-device metadata.
4. Boot with physical SIM A. U20 is absent: esim_populated=0 blocks selecting B
   before network shutdown or any I2C writes. hardware_validated stays 0 until
   the current schematic GPIO/polarity mapping is independently checked.
5. Verify Ethernet DHCP and local authenticated LuCI, all four jacks and Wi-Fi.
6. Verify modem USB enumeration and QMI device on the router host. Confirm USB-C
   service switch cannot connect two hosts. Check modem rail during transmit.
7. Configure the actual APN and test registration/data/reconnect on the purchased
   modem and service plan. Carrier certifications do not guarantee SIM activation.
8. Enable carrier GPIO actions only after bus/address, SIM default, polarity,
   power-down sequence and reset timing are proven. Current hard rail cycling
   needs graceful modem shutdown work before production use.
9. Verify persistence, unique credentials, LAN-only management, upgrade/recovery,
   power failure behavior and controller access. Run an extended soak test.

Firmware source tests do not prove this board works. Exact modem selection,
independent hardware audit, cross-build and prototype tests remain open.
