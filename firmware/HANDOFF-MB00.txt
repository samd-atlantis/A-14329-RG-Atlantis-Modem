WHEN THE FIRST BOARD ARRIVES
1. Follow PROGRAMMING-MB00.txt and hardware/audits/AUDIT-MB00.txt. Inspect the
   assembly/supports while unpowered; start rail tests without the modem. Resolve
   the modem supply/bulk-capacitor deviation before full modem commissioning.
   The 10 A distribution and closed-enclosure thermal tests remain separate.
2. Connect J14 USB-C for the router serial console and identify the actual HLK
   flash/RAM, bootloader and partition map. Preserve that unit's own complete
   flash, MAC/RF calibration and recovery backup. Establish recovery before writes.
   HLK-FACTORY-RECOVERY.txt lists the conditional OEM installation branches.
   The sysupgrade image has not been qualified as an OEM web-update upload.
3. Once compatible OpenWrt installation/recovery is proven, use the common-image
   per-unit bundle procedure in FACTORY-PROVISIONING.txt. Generate unique records
   privately using tools/provision.py, then tools/make_factory_bundle.py. Verify
   identity, hashes and sysupgrade -T before the qualified installation operation.
   The bare CI image intentionally has locked network login and no setup Wi-Fi;
   supplying the per-unit record is required for customer setup.
4. After successful provisioning, use Ethernet DHCP or ATECH-<serial> Wi-Fi and
   https://192.168.50.1 with the unit's unique admin password. Enter the carrier
   APN, then enable cellular. Leave J17 modem-service USB unplugged during normal
   cellular operation. U20 is absent; use physical nano-SIM A.
5. Complete acceptance-template.json with measured results, including every LAN
   jack, Wi-Fi/controller access, both USB orientations, LTE reconnect/load tests,
   saved configuration, unique authentication and recovery. Keep GPIO-control
   validation gates at zero until the corresponding bench work has passed.

The normal setup interface and modem drivers are included. An RMS replacement,
fleet VPN/cloud service and eSIM profile downloader are still future work.
