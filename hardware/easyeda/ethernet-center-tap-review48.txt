ETHERNET CENTER-TAP POPULATION REVIEW — 2026-10-29

Prototype recommendation: populate C41-C44 and C51-C54, 100 nF each.
This is an engineering recommendation, not a verified HLK-7628N mandatory requirement.

Our saved native PCB has C41-C44 between LAN1-LAN4_RX_CT and GND_ISO,
and C51-C54 between LAN1-LAN4_TX_CT and GND_ISO. All eight currently
have Add into BOM=no. They are not series signal capacitors or supply bypasses.

HanRun HR915310AE rev01 page1 exposes PHY-side RX CT at P5 and TX CT
at P3. These have no internal capacitor to ground. The separate internal
1000 pF/2 kV termination is on the cable side and does not replace them.
Source: HR915310AE-review48.pdf, manufacturer's drawing downloaded from
https://uploadcdn.oneyac.com/attachments/files/brand_pdf/hanrun/C3/FA/HR915310AE.pdf

Onion Omega2S reference schematic, page3 option1, connects BOTH PHY-side
transformer center taps together and to ground through C525=0.1 uF.
This is an MT7688-family reference, not the exact HLK-7628N module.
Our two separate 100 nF capacitors per port implement individual AC
shunts rather than Onion's shared node. They preserve DC isolation from
ground and do not apply an external DC supply to the PHY center taps.
Source: Omega2S-reference48.pdf
https://github.com/OnionIoT/Omega2/blob/master/Schematics/Omega2S-Reference-Schematic.pdf

Conflicting evidence: Hi-Link's supplied HLK-RM08A-KIT-V2.5 reference
schematic (7688A kit included in its HLK-7628N carrier download) leaves
the TF-1016DG center taps unconnected. This demonstrates a floating-tap
implementation, but is insufficient to prove capacitors mandatory or
unnecessary for our different integrated magnetics and PCB layout.
https://h.hlktech.com/Mobile/download/FDetail/34.html

Decision basis: use the existing 100 nF footprints as populated parts
for the first prototype, providing an AC common-mode return consistent
with the Onion family reference. Do not connect center taps directly to
3.3 V or ground based on generic PHY examples. Test all four ports at
10/100 Mb/s, including long cables, packet traffic and emissions before
freezing the production population. Vendor confirmation of the exact
HLK-7628N external magnetics recommendation remains desirable.

No live schematic/PCB population settings changed by this review.
assembly47 files still represent the prior DNP population and must be
regenerated if the prototype recommendation is adopted.
