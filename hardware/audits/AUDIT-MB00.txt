ELECTRICAL: mb00-electrical/electrical-audit.txt
285 explicit critical pin/net checks passed. All 544 schematic pin keys exist on the
PCB. Original schematic has 542 unambiguous agreements plus two Q3 ambiguities from
overlapping wires. The PCB assignments are correct. A separate four-record Q3
schematic correction candidate produces 544/544 matches. It is not applied to the
live EasyEDA project; review and native ERC remain needed before future sync.

ROUTING: mb00-physical/REPORT.txt
Current native copper was converted into an audit-only KiCad 9.0.7 board, with
explicit translation limits. Do NOT fabricate this audit copy. Zero copper-short,
clearance or drill errors were found under its translated rules. 17 warnings and
four unconnected reports are explained: three need the native pours omitted from
KiCad, and one is a microscopic isolated USB fragment with no pad attached.
Independent native geometry checks find all 92 non-ground pad-bearing nets
connected. The cached inner planes join all ground groups. No track/via keepout
intersections were found.
This does not replace actual signal/thermal/isolation tests or a native pour rebuild.

POUR CLEARANCE: mb00-physical/POUR-CLEARANCE-ADDENDUM.txt
Full copper-shape measurements against native cached ground pours give a
minimum primary-to-ground spacing of 8.01084 mm. That meets the nominal 8 mm
CAD target with only 0.01084 mm margin; it is not a fabrication-tolerance or
safety-certification guarantee. No explicit primary/GND_ISO rule selectors
exist, so add them and more manufacturing margin in a future revision.
Same-layer PE-to-ground spacing is at least 0.35413 mm. All 48 applicable
pour/keepout comparisons found no overlap above the numerical threshold.
An identical self-touching hole contour in both fills was normalized only
for analysis and logged. The primary-spacing result also matches a separate
conservative calculation against valid outer contours, independent of that
normalization. Actual submitted files remain unverified.

PROTOTYPE HOLD POINTS / SPECIFIC ACTIONS
1. Modem power: 116 uF nominal fitted versus Quectel's >= 470 uF recommendation.
   The existing 2.2 uH TPS62132 output network does not justify adding 470 uF
   without further validation. Start with the stock-first load-step plan and
   engineering disposition in mb00-electrical/power-addendum.txt.
2. Mains: main buses are 4.064 mm wide; terminal stems are 1.7 mm wide.
   DRC does not establish a 10 A rating.
   Measure temperature rise/contact heating for worst loading and closed enclosure;
   verify PE, upstream protection and isolation. F1 protects only the PSU branch.
3. USB/Ethernet: calculator widths match confirmed intended stackup. Unequal vias/
   connector breakouts need real high-speed USB and all-port traffic/soak testing.
4. Assembly/mechanical: NT4R1600 modem supports and M2 x 3 screws are separate items;
   ST1/ST2 are absent from JLC assembly BOM/CPL. Verify installation, terminal lead
   fit, modem seating/height, antennas, enclosure holes and cable clearances.
   The manufacturer drawing confirms nominal terminal hole/pin compatibility;
   worst-case tolerance and order-specific JLC acceptance remain open. See
   mb00-electrical/terminal-addendum.txt.
5. As-ordered reconciliation: retain actual submitted Gerbers/drills/assembly
   selections/order confirmation and compare with frozen assembly49 before
   closing this item.
6. Programming: identify memory/bootloader, preserve own calibration/MAC/flash,
   prove recovery, then qualify first programming and the per-unit fixture. Use
   firmware/PROGRAMMING-MB00.txt and acceptance-template.json.
