A-14329-MB00 terminal footprint review - 2026-10-29

Conclusion: the 1.40 mm holes match Dorabo's published nominal PCB layout for
DB142V-5.08. The actual published pin section is 1.00 x 0.50 mm, not the suspected
1.20 x 0.80 mm. This closes the nominal-size concern; it does not establish a
worst-case fit across every terminal/PCB tolerance or prove JLC accepted the
project's smaller copper lands. No frozen design files were changed.

PRIMARY PHYSICAL EVIDENCE
Dorabo customer drawing DB142V-5.08-XXP-C, REV T0-1, dated 2022-11-24, sheet 1/1,
shows pin dimensions 0.50 x 1.00 mm and PCB hole diameter 1.40 mm. Pole pitch is
5.08 mm; the side view shows 7.62 mm between the two solder-tail rows. Its table
lists 10 A / 300 V under UL/CUL groups B and D and wire range 24-14 AWG.
The manufacturer's product page independently specifies the same pin section and
hole diameter. Sources [1] and [2]. The drawing was rendered and visually checked.

SAVED BOARD COMPARISON
The frozen PCB uses project footprint 54563ecdb3b723c2 for CN1, J15 and J16.
Records e18-e23 specify six plated round holes of 1.399999994 mm, circular lands
of 1.850000110 mm, a 5.08 mm pole pitch and 7.62 mm row separation. The duplicated
pad numbers are 1/2/3 in both rows, consistent with two solder tails per pole.
The earlier footprint had approximately 1.50 mm holes and 2.30 mm lands.
Thus reducing the holes to 1.40 mm restored the drawing's nominal recommendation;
the 1.85 mm land diameter remains a project-specific change.

At nominal lead dimensions, sqrt(1.00^2 + 0.50^2) = 1.118 mm. A centered lead
therefore has 0.282 mm diametral geometric allowance in a nominal 1.40 mm hole.
This arithmetic does not include positional error, lead burrs, plating variation,
solder coating, insertion angle or a solder-filling allowance.

TOLERANCE LIMIT
The drawing's general linear tolerance for dimensions up to 6 mm is +/-0.20 mm,
referencing GB/T 14486-2008. It provides no separate tight tolerance at the metal
lead dimensions. Do not silently assume a +/-0.05 mm lead tolerance.

If the broad general tolerance is conservatively applied to both lead dimensions,
the maximum rectangle is 1.20 x 0.70 mm, whose diagonal is 1.389 mm. JLC publishes
+0.13/-0.08 mm for finished through holes: a 1.40 mm callout can finish at 1.32 mm.
That minimum hole is 0.069 mm smaller than the conservative maximum diagonal,
even before positional tolerances. Sources [1] and [3]. This is a conditional
tolerance-stack concern, not proof that the supplied terminals have those maximum
dimensions or that the manufacturer's recommended footprint is unusable.

The defensible disposition is: nominal drawing match confirmed; full tolerance
closure unresolved. Close it with actual lot pin dimensions plus finished-hole
gauging/assembly evidence, or a manufacturer clarification of solder-tail and
position tolerances. No automatic drilling or replacement footprint is authorized
by this finding. Enlarging completed plated holes would damage the barrel, and
changing future hole/land sizes requires another annulus and mains-clearance review.

JLC ASSEMBLY STATUS
JLC lists C2898700 as wave-soldered and supported by Economic and Standard PCBA.
That establishes service availability for the stock part, not acceptance of this
custom footprint. JLC's matching rules state that only selected components are
assembled. Sources [4] and [5].

The saved assembly49/assembly-review.txt explicitly records that no JLC
manufacturing approval had been obtained at the time of that export. It documents
part selection, BOM/CPL agreement and geometry checks, not an assembler response.
No order-specific acceptance of the six 1.40 mm holes / 1.85 mm lands was located
in the reviewed files. The current order may have later review records that are
not in this repository.

For the order record or first-article check, identify CN1/J15/J16 and C2898700;
confirm six finished plated holes at 1.40 mm nominal, 5.08 mm pole pitch and
7.62 mm row pitch on the 1.6 mm four-layer board. Retain evidence that the actual
lot seats without forcing, all six tails are soldered with acceptable hole fill,
and no pad/barrel damage occurred. Confirm these three parts were selected for
through-hole assembly. This is the remaining evidence request, not a message
already sent to JLC and not a claim that JLC refuses custom footprints.
