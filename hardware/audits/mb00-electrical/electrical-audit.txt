E01 - Q3 SCHEMATIC WIRE OVERLAP: CORRECTION PREPARED
Q3.3 drain is at (5710, -970); its USB_UART_OE_N wire runs downward to -870.
Q3.2 source is at (5710, -930); its GND_ISO wire runs upward to -1030.
These two collinear wires overlap and cross the opposite transistor terminals.
The saved PCB is correctly assigned Q3.3 = USB_UART_OE_N, Q3.2 = GND_ISO.
This is a schematic ambiguity; it is not evidence of an actual copper short.

create_q3_candidate.py creates a separate proposed native schematic with the drain
wire going upward to -1070 and the source wire downward to -830. Net labels
follow.
q3-correction-plan.json contains exact old/new records. Only four final records
change. Re-extracting the candidate gives all 544 pin keys matching the frozen
PCB,
zero label conflicts, and no absent pin numbers. The original release is
unchanged.
Candidate: schematic-mb00-Q3-proposed.epro2. Before future schematic-to-PCB sync,
review this change in EasyEDA and rerun native ERC/netlist comparison. The
candidate
is not automatically installed in the live project and is not the as-ordered
archive.

E02 - MODEM POWER RESERVE: BENCH HOLD / POSSIBLE HARDWARE ECO
See power-addendum.txt for the completed TPS62132 stability and PSU headroom
review. It specifically rejects automatic 470 uF addition and defines an
unapproved
single-board contingency plus measurements. The as-ordered hardware is unchanged.

The ordered modem is EC25AFFA-MINIPCIE. J8's used power, ground, SIM, USB and
reset
contacts agree with Quectel's EC25 Mini PCIe interface. No required pin
reassignment
was found. The board feeds all four 3.3 V contacts and all fourteen ground
contacts.

Quectel's Mini PCIe guide specifies a 3.0-3.6 V input, >= 2.7 A supply and >= 470
uF low-ESR
bypass; its explanation refers to 2G bursts. The AF-specific table gives 700-816
mA
TYPICAL LTE data current, not a transient maximum or a reduced-capacitance waiver.
The 100 uF guidance for bare LGA EC25 modules is a different power interface and
cannot be substituted. Reference [1], sections 3.5 and 6.6.7, pages 27 and 70-71.

Actual board: U3 TPS62132 (3.3 V, 3 A), L2 SWPA5040S2R2NT (native metadata: 5.5 A
Isat),
C23 = 22 uF plus C24 + C25 = 47 uF each plus small bypasses. Total bulk
capacitance is nominally 116 uF, reduced
by MLCC DC bias and tolerance. This differs from the published Mini PCIe guidance.
It does not prove the AF card will fail, but it prevents an unconditional power
pass.

Bring-up disposition:
- Scope 3V3_MODEM at J8 and 5V_ISO during cold start, attach, maximum-power LTE
  upload,
  reconnect, simultaneous Ethernet/Wi-Fi load and warm-enclosure operation.
- Modem input must stay within 3.0-3.6 V after startup, without brownout or USB
  resets.
  Check ripple, regulator/inductor temperature, startup and load-step stability.
- Close the deviation through documented engineering acceptance of measured
  lower-capacitance operation, seeking vendor concurrence where useful, or through
  a validated hardware ECO. A 470 uF / 10 V capacitor near J8 is only a test
  candidate; see power-addendum.txt for the stability concerns and L2/SS
  contingency.
  Added capacitance must be checked against U3 control-loop
  stability/inrush/soft-start
  and physical card/enclosure clearances; do not just bridge a large capacitor
  across
  small pads and call it production-ready. Validate the rework design and polarity
  against measured prototype results before applying it across the batch.
- Existing board has no dedicated footprint for that extra bulk capacitor.

CONTROL / SERVICE CHECKS
U6 MCP23008 has address 0x20 (A0/A1/A2 low). GP0 (pin 10) = SIM_SELECT,
GP1 (pin 11) = MODEM_OFF, GP2 (pin 12) = MODEM_RESET_REQ. Reset pin 6 follows
router reset.
GP0 low selects U10 NC contacts: physical SIM A VCC/CLK/RST/IO. R32 pulls low.
U20 is not needed to use SIM A. It is DNP in the assembly pair. The actual J9 is
XKNANO-1306 / C381072, a hinged NANO-SIM holder, not a micro-SIM holder.

MODEM_OFF high drives Q1, grounding U3 EN and disabling U8 USB switch. Default
pulldowns keep this low and R20 pulls EN high: the modem starts before firmware.
Thus initial firmware must not change SIM_SELECT on a live modem. A warm router
restart may retain expander state. Read/validate state before making outputs
active.
Never use a rail cut as the normal shutdown sequence without a graceful modem
shutdown; keep forced power-cycle as an explicit recovery action.

MODEM_RESET_REQ high drives Q2 and pulls J8.22 PERST# low. Q2 is open-drain; it
does not impose a router 3.3 V high level onto the modem. SW4 manually grounds
this
net. Use a 150-460 ms pulse for EC25, then release; do not treat it as a held boot
pin.
J8.44 SIM detect is unused; hot-plug detection must remain disabled and SIM
removal
requires power off. DTR is unused: leave automatic modem sleep disabled initially.
These interface facts were checked against [1]/[2].

USB mux U8 mapping is correct: common pins 8 (D+) and 7 (D-) connect to the modem.
Channel 1, pins 1 (D+) and 2 (D-), connects to the HLK. Channel 2, pins 3 (D+)
and 4 (D-), connects to J17. Q4/Q5 select channel 1 without modem-service VBUS
and channel 2 when service VBUS is present. This disconnects the HLK host while
a PC is connected. MODEM_OFF high disables both channels. Verify this transition
on the bench.
No USB-C VBUS net is tied to 5V_ISO: neither USB connector powers the entire
board.

Router console: U16 CH340C TX -> R95 -> U18.2 -> U18.6 -> HLK RX (pin 32).
HLK TX (pin 31) -> U18.5 -> U18.3 -> R96 -> U16 RX. SN74LVC2G125 pin 3 is 2Y and
pin 6 is 1Y; the asymmetric pin ordering is correct. Q3 enables the buffer only
when
J14 VBUS is present. VCC and V3 of CH340C share USB_UART_3V3. J14/J17 duplicate
USB 2.0 contacts are tied appropriately and each has two separate 5.1 kohm CC
pulldowns.
Test enumeration and serial communication with both connector orientations.
TC2050 H1/J12 pin assignments match the schematic; these are custom recovery
pinouts, NOT an ARM debugger pinout. Use the documented adapter wiring.

ROUTER / ETHERNET / POWER CHECKS
The upstream HLK GPIO38 reset-key definition does not match MB00. U1 pin 8
(WDT_RST_N/GPIO38) is NC. SW3 is pin 30, named GPIO0 on the module but MT7628
GPIO11. SW1 is hardware reset PORST_N pin 14. Wi-Fi LED pin 33 / GPIO44 is also
NC.
Remove inherited GPIO38 reset-key and unused GPIO44 LED assignments from the
MB00 device tree; otherwise an unrelated input could invoke reset behavior.

U1 pins 24/25 correctly carry I2C SCL/SDA; OpenWrt pinmux must enable I2C. Pins
31/32
are UART0, not UART1. Pins 38-53 map to PHY1-4 in manufacturer order, including
port 2/4 RX-before-TX ordering. RJ45 TX/RX and CT pins agree with the HanRun
drawing.
Four green LEDs have 470-ohm current resistors to 3V3_ROUTER. All eight CT
capacitors
are now populated in BOM and connect their individual CT nets to GND_ISO.

CT population remains an engineering choice pending PHY vendor confirmation and
10/100 link/traffic/EMC tests: the Hi-Link kit leaves taps floating while the
Onion
MT7688-family reference AC-couples them. These are not identical references for
our complete assembly. No direct 3.3 V or DC-ground CT connection has been
introduced.

Both TPS62132 pin maps follow [5]: VOS on the output, SW through inductors,
AVIN/PVIN from 5 V, FB/AGND/PGND/exposed pads grounded. FSW and DEF are low:
nominal 3.3 V at approximately 2.5 MHz (not 1.25 MHz). SS is 3.3 nF.
Stability/layout and
thermal tests remain required; nominal component values alone are insufficient.

CN1, J15, J16 L/N/PE are parallel; F1 protects only the PSU branch. Both
controller
feeds are ahead of F1. Do not interpret the 1 A PSU fuse as 10 A distribution
protection.
The installed fuse cartridge is a separately fitted item; C3131 is only the
holder.
PE continuity/fault current, mains isolation, terminal soldering, 10 A temperature
rise
and the actual JLC copper stack/weights require the physical audit and bench
tests.

The Dorabo DB142V-5.08 terminals (C2898700) have a documented UL/CUL rating of
10 A [10]. CN1 carries the TOTAL input current for J15, J16 and the gateway's
PS1 supply; its rating is not 10 A of controller output plus the gateway load.
Do not promise the full requested 10 A external load on this input terminal.
Derate the combined controller-feed allowance after measuring true-RMS CN1
current and terminal/copper temperature rise with worst-case gateway operation,
line voltage and enclosure temperature. Verify upstream fault protection too.
The PSU's typical AC current is not a guaranteed maximum, and real power divided
by line voltage does not establish RMS current for these loads. No fixed 9.5 A
or other external-feed guarantee is established here; see power-addendum.txt.

U1 BOM is HLK-7628N C209920. Hi-Link documents both 32 MB flash / 128 MB RAM and
16 MB flash / 64 MB RAM versions under this family [6]. C209920's saved attributes
do
not identify memory capacity. Read the fitted part/boot log and back up flash
before
choosing/programming an image. A 32 MB image must not be assumed safe for 16 MB.
