A-14329-MB00 physical/routing audit — 2026-10-29

Result: available saved-design checks completed. No pad-bearing open net or
copper short was found by the complementary checks below. This is a prototype
audit, NOT a statement that the ordered assembly is electrically qualified or
that the actual submitted fabrication files match this design.

SOURCE AND METHOD
The source is the frozen release pcb-assembly49.epro2, SHA256
6c2ec86f674f42a77902b4325106aed0b5708db34972f8ae38958b4818a0f21b.
The audit extracts this native archive directly. It does not use the old rev-a
KiCad concept. Native design/release files were not modified by this audit.

audit.py creates an AUDIT-ONLY KiCad 9.0.7 board from the current copper geometry:
103 components, 553 electrical/test pads, 3382 straight copper segments,
305 through vias, 34 NPTH holes, the board outline and two NT4 copper rings.
Zero-diameter native hole placeholders become SMT pads; slot relative rotations
are preserved. The generated KiCad board must NEVER be sent for fabrication.

KiCad omits native poured ground copper, keepouts, silkscreen, courtyard, solder
mask/paste rules and 3D models. These are explicit translation limits, not things
that were removed from the real board. A separate native-geometry script checks
the cached ground pours and track/via keepouts. This is not a KiCad schematic ERC
or full EasyEDA-to-KiCad migration.

REPRODUCIBLE RESULTS
1. KiCad DRC, kicad-drc.json: zero clearance/short/drill/annulus errors under the
   audit rules. The rules check a 0.15 mm signal floor, 3.2 mm primary-to-primary/PE
   and 8 mm primary-to-secondary spacing, with 0.001 mm conversion allowance.
   This floor is not a complete port of every net-specific EasyEDA rule.
   There are 17 warnings: 13 dangling tracks, 3 dangling vias, one copper sliver.
   There are four unconnected-item reports, explained below. Do not summarize
   this as an unqualified "KiCad zero-DRC" result.
2. Native net-island screen: all 92 non-ground nets have at most one pad-bearing
   connected group. The 24 standalone test/recovery pads are included.
3. Three KiCad missing connections are GND_ISO groups whose native ground pours
   were not translated. Native geometry has four ground groups without pours;
   adding the cached ground fills joins them into one group. Both inner-layer
   fills have one outer contour and holes (206 and 218 holes respectively).
   69 of 71 ground via centers lie in both filled planes. All three vias that
   KiCad marked dangling are among those 69. The two others are joined through
   routed ground. This supports ground connectivity, but is not a regenerated
   pour, a field solver, or dielectric/safety verification.
4. The fourth KiCad missing connection is native fragment 458618fd5813397f:
   USB_HOST_DP at native (1691.5226, 1573.8838) to (1691.5226, 1573.9552) mil,
   only 0.001814 mm long, no pad attached, isolated from the functional route.
   U1.54 to U8.1 is connected. Remove the orphan from a future layout revision;
   it is not evidence that the router-to-modem USB signal is open.
5. Remaining dangling-track warnings include redundant/overshooting 5 V and
   ROUTER_GPIO0_N copper. No separate pad-bearing island results. Treat these
   as cleanup candidates, not automatic functional opens. The generic B.Cu
   sliver warning has no item/coordinate in KiCad's report, so it cannot be
   localized from that report alone.
6. Independent native screen found zero track/via intersections with all
   TRACK-prohibited regions, including mounting/Tag-Connect hole clearances.

GROUND-POUR CLEARANCE
The separate full-shape audit in POUR-CLEARANCE-ADDENDUM.txt checks the native
cached fills omitted from KiCad. Minimum primary copper-to-ground distances
are 18.14894 mm for AC_L_IN, 35.52139 mm for AC_L_FUSED and 8.01084 mm for AC_N.
The last value passes the nominal 8 mm design target by only 0.01084 mm. This
is CAD geometry, not a guarantee after fabrication tolerances or a safety
certification. Verify the submitted files; add manufacturing margin in a
future revision.

No explicit primary/GND_ISO SAFE selectors exist in the native rules. Add
those selectors before the next native pour rebuild and DRC. Same-layer
PE-to-ground minimum is 0.35413 mm, above the general 0.2 mm floor; there is
no dedicated PE/GND rule. All 48 applicable pour/keepout area comparisons
found no overlap above the stated numerical threshold.

One self-touching hole contour appears in both cached fills. Its topology
normalization for analysis is fully logged; the area change is below
5e-16 square millimeter. Primary-clearance results independently match
measurements against the valid outer contours with all holes ignored, so
they do not depend on that normalization. The source design was not changed.

SIGNAL INTEGRITY
A prior JLC calculator result is saved at
hardware/easyeda/impedance-jlc-7628-calculator.png. It shows JLC04161H-7628,
four layers, 1.6 mm board thickness, 1 oz outer copper and 0.5 oz inner copper.
With L1 referenced to L2, a 6 mil pair gap gives 9.74 mil width for 90 ohm USB
and 7.19 mil width for 100 ohm Ethernet. Current USB traces are 9.74 mil
(apart from the orphan); Ethernet is 7.19 or 7.2047 mil. Native rule names still say CALC_PENDING,
but the screenshot supports the chosen dimensions for that specific stackup.

All pair conductor length SUM differences are <= 0.1411 mm; worst Ethernet sum
difference is 0.0575 mm. These are not endpoint electrical propagation delays:
USB service/console have branches and D+ has 4 vias versus 2 on D-. Several
Ethernet pairs also have unequal via counts. Sum-matched copper is not proof
of equal delay or acceptable eye diagrams. USB_HOST and USB_MODEM have matched via counts.

The native plane projection screen samples every 0.25 mm at trace centers.
It flags approximately 1.2–7.9 mm cumulative unsupported projection per conductor,
mainly connector breakout/antipad regions. The largest single flagged segment
is 1.768 mm at LAN4_RXN near the RJ45. This is a review screen, not an SI failure
threshold; it does not model return-current spreading or differential fields.
USB enumeration at high speed, modem transfer under load, all four Ethernet
ports at 100 Mbps, cable-orientation tests and traffic/error counters remain
required on the board. The user confirmed the order intent: four layers,
1.6 mm board thickness, 1 oz outer copper and JLC04161H-7628. This matches the
calculator configuration. The actual submitted manufacturing files and order
stackup have not been independently verified.

MAINS DISTRIBUTION / 10 A LIMIT
Measured main L/N/PE bus width is 4.064 mm, not 8–10 mm. The terminal stems are
1.7 mm. Each complete L/N stem is about 13 mm long, with both solder tails of its
terminal pole tapping into it. The nearest tail to the main bus is about 5.1 mm
from the bus centerline. A thin 1.016 mm trace is the PSU branch, not the panel
feed. F1 is in that PSU branch; it does not fuse the parallel controller feeds.

Illustrative cold resistance only, using copper resistivity of
1.724e-8 ohm*m and a thickness of 35 micrometers:
 - 1.7 mm wide x 5.1 mm long: 1.48 milliohms; 0.148 W if 10 A flows through
   that segment.
 - 1.7 mm wide x 13 mm long: 3.77 milliohms; 0.377 W if 10 A flows through
   all of it.
 - 4.064 mm wide x 18.4 mm long: 2.23 milliohms; 0.223 W at 10 A.
Do not add all segment heat values indiscriminately: loads divide between
outputs and the two terminal tails, and PE normally carries no load current.
These estimates exclude contact/barrel/solder resistance, current crowding,
copper temperature coefficient and heat transfer; they are NOT a temperature
rise simulation or current rating. The input terminal and both output-loading
extremes require measured temperature rise inside the closed enclosure.
External <= 10 A protection and protective-earth fault/ground-bond verification
are still required. Native clearance rules alone do not establish a safety
standard, pollution degree, material group, altitude or certification.

TERMINALS AND MECHANICALS
CN1/J15/J16 use six round 1.85 mm copper pads and 1.4 mm finished holes, 5.08 mm pole
pitch, 7.62 mm between the two tails of each pole. Nominal radial annulus 0.225 mm;
adjacent copper land gap 3.23 mm. These meet the previously selected dimensional
targets, but the actual DORABO terminal lead fit and solder fill must be checked.

Native outline is 123.952 mm square with 4 mm corner chamfers. Four enclosure
mounting holes are 6.35 mm NPTH on 107.95 mm square centers, 8.001 mm edge offsets.
This matches the supplied Integra 6 x 6 back-panel envelope. It is not a measured
fit with the actual enclosure interior, bosses, lid, cable glands or cable bends.

Modem support centers are 24.2 mm apart; host relief holes 2.2 mm. NT4 top copper
ring is 4.5 mm outside / 2.7 mm inside diameter, with mask opening and segmented
paste apertures present. JAE drawing supports shoulder 3.55 mm, pilot top 4.15 mm,
body OD 4 mm, pilot OD 2.3 mm and M2 x 0.4 thread. The socket housing height
of 5.9 mm is not the support height. The intended orientation is the pilot upward
into the modem mounting hole/notch, with the flat 4 mm body base soldered to the
host land; the 2.2 mm host hole is screw relief, not a pilot-fit hole. This is
consistent with the JAE pairing table (page 2), nut dimensions (page 7) and
2.6 mm module holes (page 8), but the brochure does not show an assembled NT4
cross-section. Its page 9 mounting diagram is for the separate plastic latch.
Confirm this mechanical stack on the purchased parts before tightening screws.
Two NT4R1600 supports plus two M2 x 3 screws are separate assembly items:
ST1/ST2 are NOT actual components in the saved 101-part JLC BOM/CPL, despite
their lands/paste and notes. Do not assume JLC automatically fitted them.
Check the selected EC25-AF Mini PCIe sample fit, antenna leads and card removal.
