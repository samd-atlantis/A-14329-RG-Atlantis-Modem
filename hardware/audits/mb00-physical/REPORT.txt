A-14329-MB00 physical/routing audit — 2026-10-29

Result: available saved-design checks completed. No pad-bearing open net or
copper short was found by the complementary checks below. This is a prototype
audit, NOT a statement that the ordered assembly is electrically qualified or
that the actual submitted fabrication files match this design.

SOURCE AND METHOD
The source is the frozen release pcb-assembly49.epro2, SHA256
6c2ec86f674f42a77902b4325106aed0b5708db34972f8ae38958b4818a0f21b.
The audit extracts this native archive directly. It does not use the old rev-a
KiCad concept. Native design/release files were not modified by this audit.

audit.py creates an AUDIT-ONLY KiCad 9.0.7 board from the current copper geometry:
103 components, 553 electrical/test pads, 3382 straight copper segments,
305 through vias, 34 NPTH holes, the board outline and two NT4 copper rings.
Zero-diameter native hole placeholders become SMT pads; slot relative rotations
are preserved. The generated KiCad board must NEVER be sent for fabrication.

KiCad omits native poured ground copper, keepouts, silkscreen, courtyard, solder
mask/paste rules and 3D models. These are explicit translation limits, not things
that were removed from the real board. A separate native-geometry script checks
the cached ground pours and track/via keepouts. This is not a KiCad schematic ERC
or full EasyEDA-to-KiCad migration.

REPRODUCIBLE RESULTS
1. KiCad DRC, kicad-drc.json: zero clearance/short/drill/annulus errors under the
   audit rules. The rules check a 0.15mm signal floor, 3.2mm primary-to-primary/PE
   and 8mm primary-to-secondary spacing, with 0.001mm conversion allowance.
   This floor is not a complete port of every net-specific EasyEDA rule.
   There are 17 warnings: 13 dangling tracks, 3 dangling vias, one copper sliver.
   There are four unconnected-item reports, explained below. Do not summarize
   this as an unqualified "KiCad zero-DRC" result.
2. Native net-island screen: all 92 non-ground nets have at most one pad-bearing
   connected group. The 24 standalone test/recovery pads are included.
3. Three KiCad missing connections are GND_ISO groups whose native ground pours
   were not translated. Native geometry has four ground groups without pours;
   adding the cached ground fills joins them into ONE group. Both inner-layer
   fills have one outer contour and holes (206 and 218 holes respectively).
   69 of 71 ground via centers lie in BOTH filled planes. All three vias that
   KiCad marked dangling are among those 69. The two others are joined through
   routed ground. This supports ground connectivity, but is not a regenerated
   pour, a field solver, or dielectric/safety verification.
4. The fourth KiCad missing connection is native fragment458618fd5813397f:
   USB_HOST_DP at native (1691.5226,1573.8838) to (1691.5226,1573.9552) mil,
   only 0.001814mm long, no pad attached, isolated from the functional route.
   U1.54 to U8.1 is connected. Remove the orphan from a future layout revision;
   it is not evidence that the router-to-modem USB signal is open.
5. Remaining dangling-track warnings include redundant/overshooting 5V and
   ROUTER_GPIO0_N copper. No separate pad-bearing island results. Treat these
   as cleanup candidates, not automatic functional opens. The generic B.Cu
   sliver warning has no item/coordinate in KiCad's report, so it cannot be
   localized from that report alone.
6. Independent native screen found zero track/via intersections with all
   TRACK-prohibited regions, including mounting/Tag-Connect hole clearances.

SIGNAL INTEGRITY
There IS a prior JLC calculator result, hardware/easyeda/
impedance-jlc-7628-calculator.png. It shows JLC04161H-7628, four layers, 1.6mm,
outer1oz/inner0.5oz, L1 referenced toL2: 6mil pair gap gives9.74mil for90ohmUSB
and7.19mil for100ohmEthernet. Current USB traces are9.74mil (apart from the
orphan); Ethernet is7.19 or7.2047mil. Native rule names still say CALC_PENDING,
but the screenshot supports the chosen dimensions for that specific stackup.

All pair conductor length SUM differences are <=0.1411mm; worst Ethernet sum
difference is0.0575mm. These are not endpoint electrical propagation delays:
USB service/console have branches and D+has4vias versusD-has2. Several Ethernet
pairs also have unequal via counts. Sum-matched copper is not proof of equal
delay or acceptable eye diagrams. USB_HOST and USB_MODEM have matched via counts.

The native plane projection screen samples every0.25mm at trace centers.
It flags approximately1.2–7.9mm cumulative unsupported projection per conductor,
mainly connector breakout/antipad regions. The largest single flagged segment
is1.768mm at LAN4_RXN near the RJ45. This is a review screen, not an SI failure
threshold; it does not model return-current spreading or differential fields.
USB enumeration at high speed, modem transfer under load, all four Ethernet
ports at100Mbps, cable-orientation tests and traffic/error counters remain
required on the board. Confirm the actual JLC order used the intended stackup.

MAINS DISTRIBUTION / 10A LIMIT
Measured main L/N/PE bus width is4.064mm, NOT8–10mm. The terminal stems are
1.7mm. Each complete L/N stem is about13mm long, with BOTH solder tails of its
terminal pole tapping into it. The nearest tail to the main bus is about5.1mm
from the bus centerline. A thin1.016mm trace is the PSU branch, not the panel
feed. F1 is in that PSU branch; it does not fuse the parallel controller feeds.

Illustrative cold resistance only, using copper rho=1.724e-8ohm*m and35um:
 - 1.7mm wide x5.1mm long: 1.48mohm;0.148W if10A flows through that segment.
 - 1.7mm wide x13mm long: 3.77mohm;0.377W if10A flows through all of it.
 - 4.064mm wide x18.4mm long: 2.23mohm;0.223W at10A.
Do not add all segment heat values indiscriminately: loads divide between
outputs and the two terminal tails, and PE normally carries no load current.
These estimates exclude contact/barrel/solder resistance, current crowding,
copper temperature coefficient and heat transfer; they are NOT a temperature
rise simulation or current rating. The input terminal and both output-loading
extremes require measured temperature rise inside the closed enclosure.
External <=10A protection and protective-earth fault/ground-bond verification
are still required. Native clearance rules alone do not establish a safety
standard, pollution degree, material group, altitude or certification.

TERMINALS AND MECHANICALS
CN1/J15/J16 use six round1.85mm copper pads and1.4mm finished holes,5.08mm pole
pitch,7.62mm between the two tails of each pole. Nominal radial annulus0.225mm;
adjacent copper land gap3.23mm. These meet the previously selected dimensional
targets, but the actual DORABO terminal lead fit and solder fill must be checked.

Native outline is123.952mm square with4mm corner chamfers. Four enclosure
mounting holes are6.35mmNPTH on107.95mm square centers,8.001mm edge offsets.
This matches the supplied Integra6x6/back-panel envelope. It is not a measured
fit with the actual enclosure interior, bosses, lid, cable glands or cable bends.

Modem support centers are24.2mm apart; host relief holes2.2mm. NT4 top copper
ring is4.5mm outside/2.7mm inside diameter, with mask opening and segmented
paste apertures present. JAE drawing supports shoulder3.55mm, pilot top4.15mm,
bodyOD4mm, pilotOD2.3mm, M2x0.4. Socket housing height5.9mm is not the support
height. TwoNT4R1600 supports plus twoM2x3 screws are separate assembly items:
ST1/ST2 are NOT actual components in the saved101-part JLC BOM/CPL, despite
their lands/paste and notes. Do not assume JLC automatically fitted them.
Check the selected EC25-AF MiniPCIe sample fit, antenna leads and card removal.
