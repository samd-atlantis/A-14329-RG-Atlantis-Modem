A-14329-MB00 native ground-pour clearance addendum — 2026-10-29

This closes the ground-pour geometry omission in the audit-only KiCad copy.
The frozen assembly49 source hash is unchanged:
6c2ec86f674f42a77902b4325106aed0b5708db34972f8ae38958b4818a0f21b.
No native layout, release file or ordered manufacturing file was changed.

PRIMARY COPPER TO GND_ISO
Full copper-shape distances were measured to both cached inner ground fills,
including primary tracks, terminal pads, fuse pads and PSU input pads. The
primary nets contain no vias. All-layer XY projections were checked, so a
track being on an outer layer did not exempt it from this conservative screen.

Net          Minimum distance    Closest primary feature
AC_L_IN      18.148938 mm        F1 pad 1
AC_L_FUSED   35.521391 mm        PS1 pad 2
AC_N          8.010838 mm        Diagonal neutral branch to PS1

The controlling AC_N track is ac_dis22d4426c19829142, 1.016 mm wide. Its
nearest copper edge is at (40.831181, 30.574742) mm. The ground corner is at
(43.281600, 38.201600) mm, in native board XY coordinates. A separate analytic
point-to-segment calculation, subtracting half the trace width, reproduces
the 8.010838 mm result. Thus the saved geometry meets the nominal 8 mm target.

The narrowest margin is only 0.010838 mm beyond 8 mm. This is a nominal CAD
result, not a guarantee that manufacturing tolerances preserve 8 mm. Confirm
the actual submitted Gerbers and JLC process allowances. For a future revision,
give this diagonal-to-ground-corner spacing more manufacturing margin.

RULE COVERAGE GAP
The native PRIMARY_SECONDARY_8MM rule has many primary/secondary selectors,
but no explicit AC_L_IN, AC_L_FUSED or AC_N selector against GND_ISO. The only
GND_ISO selector is an empty single-net selector. Consequently, native zero-DRC
alone did not establish the primary-to-ground-pour spacing. The measurements
above check it independently. Add explicit primary/GND selectors in a future
revision before rebuilding pours and running native DRC.

PROTECTIVE EARTH TO GROUND
Minimum PE-to-GND distance on the SAME copper layer is 0.354130 mm, between
J14 shield pad 4 and the layer 16 ground fill. This exceeds the general 0.2 mm
clearance floor. There is no dedicated PE/GND spacing selector. This result
does not establish a safety-isolation classification between PE and GND.
Some outer-layer PE traces overlap ground planes in XY projection; their
layer separation is through PCB dielectric. That projection overlap is not
a same-layer electrical short and was not treated as one.

POUR / KEEPOUT INTERSECTIONS
All 48 applicable comparisons of the two ground fills against regions that
prohibit COPPER, FILL or PLANE were checked using full areas. No overlap above
0.0001 square millimeter was found. This includes enclosure-mounting, modem-
support and Tag-Connect hole clearance regions, plus the isolation rectangles.
Component-only and TRACK-only regions were not misinterpreted as pour bans.
The earlier zero track/via keepout result is a separate check.

GEOMETRY METHOD AND LIMITS
audit_pour_clearance.py uses Shapely 2.1.2 / GEOS. Rectangles are full polygons;
round pads and vias are circles; tracks and oval pads are stadium shapes.
For a stadium, distance(axis, filled polygon) minus the radius gives its full
copper-edge separation; the reported values are not centerline clearances.
Native cached-fill coordinates are scaled by 10 native mil per cached unit.
Circular fill arcs are tessellated with at most 0.00001 mm chord sagitta.

Both cached fills contain the same self-touching hole contour near
(101.699029, 13.139398) mm. GEOS make_valid normalizes that hole for analysis;
the area change is less than 5e-16 square millimeter. This is logged in
pour-clearance.json, not silently changed in the source. Both outer contours
are valid. Independently measuring primary spacing against those outer
contours while ignoring ALL holes produces the same minimum distances.
Ignoring holes treats the whole outer contour as copper and can only reduce
or preserve spacing. The matching primary-spacing result therefore does not
depend on the normalization.

The closest PE feature and keepout regions do not lie at the normalized
self-touching contour. Nevertheless, regenerate and inspect native pours in
the next layout revision. This analysis does not validate dielectric withstand,
surface creepage under a selected safety standard, field performance, or the
actual ordered Gerber data.
